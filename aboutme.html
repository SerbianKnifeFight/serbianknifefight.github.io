about me

Hello, welcome to my very unorganized and rambling 'about me' page. My name is June, i'm 17 years old, from Serbia. I like physics and mathematics (especially geometry), quantum computers, electronics, computer history, hardware, 3D graphics and rendering, low-level programming, operating systems, game engines, and game development. I'm also into 2D and 3D art, drawing, painting, reading, music theory and composition, YTPMV, Siivagunner ('high quality video game rips'), philosophy, Marxism, chess, modding, gaming, collecting, working out, and watching NFL, and that's already far too many things. I plan on studying Electrical Engineering, specifically physical electronics or nuclear energetics. I've been playing piano since i was 8 and i'd love to start learning saxophone, my second favorite instrument (right behind piano, of course :)). The programming languages i'm most comfortable in are C, C++ and Lua. I also love reading (see the reading section for the books). The most influential work in my life is Homestuck, though besides that i don't read much fiction. One of my main literary interests is Marxism, and i'm a Left-Communist myself.

I'm one of the co-creators of fishyogurtbacteriavirus, a creative group that focuses on video game projects. In my free time (i don't have a lot of it) i play piano and make YTPMV and mashup/cover music, sometimes even original music! I've been a participant of the Technical Sciences program at Petnica Research Center since 2026. I love collecting old hardware of any type: peripherals, old terminals, computers, electronic instruments, and so on. I compete in mathematics and physics olympiads in my country (Serbia) and have won multiple medals. I still love playing games (see below), but nowadays i don't play a whole lot; 90% of the time i'm playing Chess or Super Smash Bros. Melee.
