about me

welcome to the about page, i'm trying to tidy it up. i'm June, 17 years old, from Serbia. I write code across a few languages and mostly put that toward building games, i also like editing videos.

i like physics and mathematics, quantum computing, electronics, computer history, hardware, 3D graphics and rendering, low-level programming, operating systems, game engines, and game development generally. Beyond that, 2D and 3D art, drawing, painting, reading, music theory and composition, YTPMV, Siivagunner(high quality video game rips), philosophy, chess, modding, collecting, lifting, and watching NFL on the side. It's a lot, i know

On paper my occupation is "high school student." i've played piano since i was eight and i'm hoping to pick up saxophone next (a close second favorite behind piano, naturally :)). The languages i'm most at home in are C, C++, and Lua. i read more than most people expect of a programmer, though the one work that's actually shaped how i live my life is Homestuck, not much other fiction comes close.

I co-founded fishyogurtbacteriavirus, a small creative collective built around game projects. When i have the time (which isn't often) i play piano or put together YTPMV and mashup covers. Since 2026 i've been part of the Technical Sciences program at Petnica Research Center. I collect old hardware - peripherals, terminals, computers, electronic instruments, anything with history behind it. I compete in math and physics olympiads in my country and have taken home a few medals. I'm kind of a gamer, just less than i used to, these days it's mostly chess or Super Smash Bros. Melee.
